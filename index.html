🔊 How Text-to-Speech Works Offline
On Android, open Settings → System → Languages & input → Text-to-speech output → Preferred engine (Speech Recognition & Synthesis from Google) and download Sindhi (Pakistan) or Urdu for native offline script voices. Even without a Sindhi pack installed, you can switch the sidebar dropdown to Syllabic Final-Vowel Enunciator so any English/Hindi voice pronounces every Sindhi syllable and final short vowel (-u, -a, -i) clearly!
📱 Using Offline on Android (Zero Internet)
1. Save sindhi_teaching_grammar.html to your phone’s Downloads folder.
2. Anytime you have zero internet (or are in Airplane Mode), open the Files by Google app, tap Downloads, tap sindhi_teaching_grammar.html, and choose Chrome.
3. All your quiz answers and current chapter page are automatically saved in local browser storage!
